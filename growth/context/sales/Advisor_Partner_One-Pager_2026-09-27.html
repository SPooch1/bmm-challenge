More plan information is not the fix on its own. About half of participants already say they get more than they can absorb, and much of it goes unread. Bend Your Baseline is built around small actions people can repeat, rather than more to read.

It gives your plans something to do, not something to read. Employees start with a 21-day challenge on their phone or computer, a few minutes a day, then move into one monthly module for the rest of the year. Each step asks the employee to do something, not just read something.

What employees do in the first 21 days
Money
Open a Peace of Mind Account, a separate cushion set aside for the next surprise expense.
Health
One simple daily action for the body, kept small enough for a busy week.
Stress
A two-minute breathing exercise they can use when stress hits.

Built for employers with a 401(k) and roughly 10 to 250 employees. Your people get plenty of information already. Plan materials, benefit guides, newsletters. More information does not always make the next step easier for someone who is stretched thin.

Bend Your Baseline starts with something to do, not something to read. Employees take a few minutes a day for 21 days, and each day asks for one small action they can choose to keep using after the challenge ends.
What HR does
Picks a start date and forwards one email with one link.
No employee list. Employees sign themselves up. You never send names or emails.
20 minutes at kickoff, 30 at the review. Scott runs the group from there.
What employees do: three daily targets
Money
Start a Peace of Mind Account, a separate savings account set aside for the next surprise bill.
Health
One simple daily health action, small enough to keep doing in a busy week.
Stress
4x4x4x4 breathing, a two-minute reset they can use when stress hits.

What HR sees
Who joined, how far along each person is, and whether they are still checking in. Then a Day-21 summary with group averages.
The Day-21 summary covers participation, completion, and the group's average start and end check-ins. Scott keeps the admin view; HR gets the Day-21 summary.
Never any one person's stress ratings, savings amounts, notes or survey answers.

The call

Build it now as the way into plan conversations. Sell it as a product in 2027, after the first groups finish.

Your December target is 4 to 10 plans. So every 21-day pilot this fall goes to an employer that has a 401(k) you would want, and success means a plan conversation plus a finished group. A group that finishes gives you what every competitor has and you lack: your own numbers.

Where it stands

What is strong, and what has to be true before an outside company logs in

Strong today

  • One action for everyone, no menu. The employee never has to pick where to start.
  • Starts with a win (the Peace of Mind Account) before any education.
  • Health, money and stress in one program, aimed at turnover, productivity and health costs, not plan leakage.
  • HR forwards one link. No employee list changes hands.
  • Day 22 and after keeps going (Keep Your Margin, 90/180/365-day check-ins), which is what makes a yearly license possible.
  • The book (Book 1) and the keynote give it a public face competitors cannot copy.

Gaps

  • No finished group yet. No outcome claims anywhere until one finishes.
  • Privacy in the first 21 days. Today a company admin could read daily entries and survey answers by name. Fix built and reviewed (see below), not yet live. Until it is, no HR logins.
  • Seat limit. Shown on screen but not enforced. Fix built, not yet live.
  • One person. You run every group by hand, and there is no multi-company admin.
  • The Roadmap had no delivery. Months 2 to 12 ran on ProductDyno, retired 8/26. Now built into the app (see below), not yet live.
  • Website. The Roadmap page made claims that were not true or broke your rules. Fixed in the new site build.

Why these numbers: a flat price per company is easier for a small employer to say yes to than a per-employee rate. Hermes' $3 to $5 per employee per month comes to $900 to $1,500 a year at 25 people, which sat under its own $3,000 minimum. The tiers work out to roughly $30 to $150 per employee per year, below the coaching platforms because there is no one-on-one coaching. Your time per group is my estimate, not measured: about 4 hours (setup, kickoff, weekly check, Day-21 review). Measure it on the first pilot and reprice if it runs long. Benchmarks found: BrightPlan $41.64 to $71.40 per employee per year with planner access (Sourcewell, 2023); EBRI 2018 found 43% of large employers spent $50 or less per employee per year. The smallest tier runs above BrightPlan per head because it carries your time on a small group.

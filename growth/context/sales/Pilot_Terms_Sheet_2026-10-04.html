Free Pilot: What It Includes

The plain-language terms for one 21-day group of up to 25 employees. Zero cost, zero obligation.
Workplace Financial Solutions
Scott Pooch
Retirement Plan Consultant and Educator
What the pilot includes
The 21-Day Challenge for one group with one shared start date, on the Build More Margin app (phone or computer, nothing to download from an app store).
Up to 25 employee seats. Employees join from one link.
Three daily targets: a Peace of Mind Account (money), one daily health action (body), and 4x4x4x4 breathing (stress).
A ready-to-send launch kit: the announcement email, reminders, and a Slack or Teams version.
A Day-21 summary and a 30-minute review meeting.
Zero cost to the employer and to employees.
What we need from HR
A start date.
One forwarded email with the join link, sent to the employees you choose to invite.
20 minutes at kickoff and 30 minutes at the review.
The number of people you invited, so the summary can show how many started.
No employee list. You never send names, emails, or payroll data.
Seat limit
Up to 25 employees can join the pilot.
Timeline
Kickoff call20 minutes. We pick the start date, you get the join link and the launch kit.
Before Day 1HR sends the announcement. Employees sign up from the link and land on the shared start date.
Day 1 to Day 21Employees spend a few minutes a day. HR sends short notes from the kit on Day 1, around Day 10, and on Day 21. Scott runs the group.
Day 21The challenge ends. The app switches to Keep Your Margin, a short daily check-in employees can choose to keep using.
Review meetingWithin about a week of Day 21. 30 minutes. We walk through the Day-21 summary together.
Privacy
What HR sees
Who joined, how far each person has gotten, and whether they are still checking in.
A Day-21 summary with group totals and group averages, including participation, completion, average stress check-ins, and average start and end self-ratings.
What HR does not see
Any one person's stress ratings, savings amounts, journal notes, or survey answers.
Anything an employee does in the app after Day 21 beyond whether they are still active.
Data use. Employee data is used to run the challenge and to prepare the group summary. It is never sold. Deleting an account. Any employee can ask to have their account and data deleted by emailing scott@scottpooch.com. Education, not advice. The challenge teaches habits. It is not financial, investment, tax, legal, or medical advice.
What happens after
No obligation. Day 21 is not a renewal date. At the review we look at the Day-21 summary, and if you want to keep going we talk through options: The Employee Roadmap (12 monthly modules, with the 21-Day Challenge as Month 1) and, where you have a 401(k), a broader Workplace Financial Solutions engagement. If you stop at the review, employees keep their accounts and the Keep Your Margin mode.

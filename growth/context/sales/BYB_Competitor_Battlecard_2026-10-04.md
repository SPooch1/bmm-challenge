# Bend Your Baseline: Competitor Battlecard

INTERNAL ONLY. Built 2026-10-04. Web sources accessed 2026-10-04 unless a source date is given. Not for prospects; never quote a competitor's numbers to a prospect as fact.

Labels: **PRIMARY** = the company's own page or contract. **SECONDARY** = press, blog, or third party. **THEIR CLAIM** = vendor-reported outcome with no method shown. **UNVERIFIED** = could not confirm on 2026-10-04.

## Our program in one line (for contrast)

Free 21-day pilot for up to 25 employees, then the 12-month Employee Roadmap. One action for everyone, starts with a win (Peace of Mind Account), health + money + stress together, aimed at the employer's P&L, HR forwards one link, small-employer friendly, run personally by Scott.
Honest weaknesses: no finished cohort and no outcome data yet; one person; no payroll or record keeper integrations; no published price; privacy field split BUILT 10/04 on the app branch privacy-split (Codex-reviewed) but NOT DEPLOYED, so no HR logins until it ships.

## Where we lose (be honest with ourselves)

1. **Price floor is zero.** Record keepers, Paychex Perks, and Payactiv come with things the employer already pays for or at no charge to the business. A standalone price has to be justified against nothing.
2. **No proof.** Every platform above publishes its own outcome claims. We have no finished cohort. Their numbers are their claims, but they still beat silence in a procurement conversation.
3. **One person.** No backup, capacity limits, no help desk. A 500-person employer will worry about that.
4. **No integrations.** No payroll, HRIS, or record keeper connections; no single sign-on.
5. **Security questionnaires.** Privacy field split built on the privacy-split branch but not yet deployed (10/04); no written security documentation; no SOC 2. Larger HR and finance teams will stop here.
6. **SmartDollar and RPAG already serve advisers.** The partner lane is not empty, and Scott's own takeover model makes some advisers wary of introducing him.
7. **Brand.** Ramsey, Fidelity, and OneDigital names do the trust work for them.

Where we can win: small employers (under ~100) with a 401(k) and an HR person who wants something that asks very little of them; rooms where Scott is in person; employers where the program is the door to a plan conversation.

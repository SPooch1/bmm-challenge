# Employer funnel notes, October 2026

Recommendations only. No sales collateral was edited. Read with `growth/context/sales/` (pilot one-pager, program plan, objection handling, employee FAQ) and the app review.

## The one idea

Three things put the program in front of individuals: the free app, the book, and the talk. None of them hands anyone to the employer pilot today. The hand-off should happen at one moment in each, and it is the same moment every time: right after the person has done something, not when they are being asked to read something. That is the program's own argument (start with a win, then information), so the funnel should follow it.

## Where the "bring this to my company" moment belongs

**In the app: Day 21 and the Day 22 card, not before.**
- Day 21 is the only day a person has a before/after of their own to look at. That is when "my team should do this" is a real thought. Put one line under the comparison: "Want your company to run this? Scott runs a free 21-day group for up to 25 employees." Link to the booking page (workplacefinancialsolutions.com/book). Individual accounts only; cohort members are already in one.
- Repeat it once on the new Day 22 "the 21 days are done" card, then never again. A nag in Keep Your Margin would cost more goodwill than it earns.
- Do not put it on Day 0 or in the welcome overlay. Nobody has a reason to recommend something they have not done.
- Measure it: a `?src=app21` tag on that link, so the owner funnel shows how many bookings start there (same counter mechanism as `src_book`).

**In the book: the back matter, in the "two ways to keep going" section, as a third way for owners and managers.**
- The book already ends with the free app and the founding group. Add a short third paragraph for readers who lead a team: the free 25-person pilot, one link, no employee list, Day-21 summary. Two or three sentences. Put it after the two individual options, so it reads as "and if you run a team" rather than a pitch.
- Not in the bonus chapter for team leaders? Yes, there too, but as one sentence at the end. The bonus chapter is where that reader already is.
- The link in the book should carry `?src=book` for the app (done in this sprint) and a separate tag for the pilot page so book-driven pilot inquiries can be counted.

**In the talk: the closing slide, as the second QR, only in HR, CPA and owner rooms.**
- Service-club rooms get the one QR (guide + app). HR, CPA and owner rooms get a second QR to the pilot page. The spoken line is the objection-handling answer to "what results have you had": no group has finished yet, you would be one of the first, zero cost, four measures agreed up front. Honesty is the pitch here, and it is the only one that holds when the room asks for numbers.
- The talk has been delivered once, to a service club. Do not describe HR-room delivery until it has happened.

## What HR sees first

The order matters more than the amount. Recommended first screen for an HR contact, in the one-pager, the kickoff call, and the demo:

1. **What employees do** (three targets, a few minutes a day). The product is the behaviour, not the dashboard.
2. **What HR has to do** (forward one link, two meetings). This answers the hidden question, "how much of my time is this?"
3. **What HR never sees** (any one person's stress, savings, notes, answers). Lead with the privacy line before they ask. The employee FAQ already does this well.
4. **What HR gets on Day 21** (participation, completion, who is still checking in, group averages when the group is large enough).
5. **Only then the dashboard.** Use `dashboard.html?demo=1` on the call. It opens in the HR view: engagement only. Switch to the owner view only if they ask what Scott sees.

Keep the pilot one-pager's two statistics (PwC 2026, J.P. Morgan 2026) as the only numbers in the conversation. Do not add funnel counts or any app usage number until a group has finished.

## Three gaps worth closing before the first pilot

1. **The employee FAQ answer for a missed day is now out of date.** It says "page back to read any day you missed." After this sprint a missed day can also be logged late, and the app shows a comeback card. Update the FAQ when the app ships (one sentence).
2. **The pilot page has no tag.** Add `?src=` to every route into the booking page (app Day 21, book back matter, talk QR, LinkedIn) so the funnel card can show which door people came through. Counts only.
3. **The Day-21 review needs a fixed agenda.** HR will ask "what next" on that call. The program plan already has the answer (the Employee Roadmap inside a Workplace Financial Solutions engagement; pricing not set). Write the 30-minute agenda once and reuse it: numbers, what employees said they are keeping, privacy recap, the one next step.

## What not to do

- No "bring this to my company" button during the 21 days. The person is the customer during the challenge; the employer is the customer after it.
- No completion rate, retention rate or "typical results" anywhere, including the demo. The demo's sample end-survey is flat on purpose.
- No cohort funnel for HR. The owner funnel is Scott's tool; HR has the per-day engagement chart for their own group, which is the honest version of the same picture.

You’ve started strong before. And eventually, you were right back where you began.

That isn’t a character flaw. It’s the yo-yo: a burst of effort on top of a baseline that stayed mostly the same. When the push ends, you fall back toward the level your everyday habits tend to support.

Bend Your Baseline is a short, practical book about what happens after the motivation wears off. Instead of another big push, it gives you 21 days of small actions aimed at your baseline in three areas: money, body, and stress.

One short chapter a day. One small action, with a smaller version for hard days. A Peace of Mind Account to start building a cushion for surprise expenses. A two-minute breathing exercise. A free companion app. And a plan for Day 22 and after.

Scott Pooch is a retirement plan consultant, educator, and speaker in Columbus, Ohio. He coached Division I distance runners for four years, which is where he learned that the easy days matter as much as the hard ones.
